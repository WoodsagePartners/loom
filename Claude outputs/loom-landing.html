The order matters

The How is the easy part to get excited about.

It's also the part that fails silently when it's aimed at the wrong step. An AI agent bolted onto a process nobody's mapped just moves the bottleneck — it doesn't remove it.

Loom holds the line on sequence: lay out the process as it actually runs today — who touches it, where it waits, where it breaks — before anyone picks a technique to fix it. That ordering is the whole product.

The data model

One company, many problems worth mapping.

Loom's shape follows how the work actually nests: a company's Workspace holds every process worth examining, each Process is mapped in Lanes, each Lane is a row of Steps joined by Lines, and every Step has a Role — the person, team, system or AI actually doing it today.

Vocabulary

Five pieces, nested in this order.

The whole model is just these five things, each one living inside the last.

Workspace

A company's own environment in Loom. Everything else — every process, every person invited — lives inside one.

holds many Processes

Process

One real sequence of work, mapped end to end — "order to delivery," "hiring a role." The unit you'd actually fix.

is made of Lanes

Lane

A horizontal row grouping Steps by stage, team or department. A process has as many Lanes as it needs.

holds an array of Steps

Step

One thing that happens — named with a verb. Steps sit in a Lane and connect to other Steps via Lines.

joined by Lines, owned by a Role

Line

Connects two Steps. Solid for work moving forward, dashed for information only, dotted red for an exception or loop back.

a smart edge between Steps

Role

Who or what does a Step today: a person, a team, a system already in place, or an AI. Shape shows the kind, color shows which one.

attaches to a Step
What happens next

A mapped Step is where the How gets to start.

Once a Step is on the map — its Role, its pain, how often it runs — it's a real candidate for change: automate it, assist it with AI, hand it to a different Role, or cut it outright. That's the conversation Struinova runs once Loom has done the honest part first.
