# The Loom — What Changed in This Build

Built 2026-07-26 against `SPEC.md`. File: `the-loom.html`.

## The question is the hero

Large at the top of the screen, with dead versions ghosted above it and struck through. The graph sits underneath rather than dominating.

When a question is retired and replaced, that is the most important event in the application, and it now looks like one.

## The canvas earns its space

A thread with no pulls collapses the canvas to zero height and shows an invitation instead. No more empty grid eating the best real estate on screen.

## The scoreboard replaced the mood bar

Measures method, not volume. In order:

- **Questions retired** — the highest value event, counted first
- **Assumptions turned to facts** — step two work made visible
- **Loose ends held** vs **loose ends pulled** — stored potential, where high held is good
- **Percent of pulls run by them** — the real ZPD fade, and the number that should climb
- **Days since it moved** — slack made numeric, turns amber past two weeks
- Live · quiet · cut counts across all threads

The handwritten line survives as the caption underneath. Deliberately absent: total pulls, ideas generated, node count. Vanity metrics teach the wrong behaviour.

A toggle in the dock, **I PULL / THEY PULL**, is how the team percentage gets captured. Flip it when a client runs the pull instead of you.

## The library learns

Every **KEEP** pushes that node's provocations into the technique's exemplars. The next pull carries them with the instruction: *provocations this facilitator judged worth keeping, match this calibre and bite, never the subject matter.*

- **Keep rate** shows per technique. Twenty percent means it is badly executed or reached for at the wrong moments.
- **Craft notes** box per technique, where you write your own instruction for how you actually run it.
- **COLD toggle** in the dock ignores the entire library and runs vanilla, so exemplars cannot calcify into sameness.
- **EXPORT LIBRARY** writes it out as its own file, independent of any engagement. It is yours and it travels.

Learning is general by design. Client material stays context for a single pull and is never promoted into craft.

**Analogous ships with both variants.** Pairwise, and **triangulation** with the two sided harvest written into its execution: what holds across three or four unrelated domains, and separately, what appears in only one, because that is the novelty candidate.

## Fibers

Other people's words, entering the thread. Paste what was said and whose voice it is. It is attributed, dated, marked as their voice, and read by every pull.

Threads with no fibers get called out. This is the smallest honest step toward the room being bigger than one person.

## Worked and quiet

A third thread state, between live and cut. Honestly attempted, nothing yielded yet, not abandoned. The scoreboard treats it as neutral. Innovation work needed somewhere to put a real dead end that is not a confession.

## The app has an opinion

One handwritten line under the question, computed locally so it costs nothing and appears instantly. It notices, it never prescribes. Examples it will surface:

- Four techniques in, and absence has never been one of them.
- Every word on this thread is yours. Nobody from the field has spoken yet.
- Eight pulls and the question has never once changed.
- Eleven loose ends sitting here, and nothing pulled in a week.
- Four things are still provisional. Nothing is part of the record until you say so.

## Techniques speak plainly

Every button in the dock carries a plain description under its name. Someone at Noble can choose one without knowing your vocabulary, which is the entire point of capability staying behind.

## Carried over intact

- **Node graph.** Each node is one technique applied. Pull twice from the same node and the thread forks. Fold any branch. Three levels of zoom detail so text is never four pixels tall.
- **Loose ends as reservoir.** Held per node, cyan dot means loose, dim means pulled. Hover to peek, click to consider, select one to pull it alone.
- **Ledger.** Append only, derived names like commit messages, full state snapshot per entry. Session Brief, and a movement report by subtraction between the last two entries.
- **Night shift.** Queue assignments, the app drafts the brief from state with objective, focus, questions, out of bounds and what a good morning looks like. You edit it, approve it, and it goes on the ledger so the shift can be held to it. Handoff ceremony with the Jung line.
- **Multiplayer capable.** Author on every node and ledger entry, participant list on every thread, nothing assumes a single writer.

## Known limits

1. Single player. Silent grouping and async contribution across rotations need a backend.
2. No web access. Fact finding reasons from training with marked confidence and names who would really know. A lead and a place to look, never a citation.
3. Verified by inspection, not by execution. The Linux sandbox was down during the build, so the syntax check and layout math were not run.
